================================================================================
🎬 STICKMAN VIDEO EDITING STUDIO (STICK STORY ENGINE)
================================================================================

Welcome to the Stickman Video Editing Studio!
This plugin is 100% focused on Stickman Storytelling, Tactical Survival Steps,
and Life Hacks shorts.

QUICK START:
1. Run selftest to verify your environment:
   python3 studio.py selftest

2. Create a new stickman short:
   python3 studio.py new stick-story ~/faceless-shorts/my-first-stick-short

3. Run full automated pipeline:
   python3 studio.py autopilot ~/faceless-shorts/my-first-stick-short --fast

KEY CAPABILITIES:
- 100% Code-Driven: No generative AI morphing, zero hallucinated pixels.
- Ink-black stick figures with expressive acting eyes and dynamic moods.
- Day and night environments with warm silhouette glows.
- Animated gold numbered step cards.
- Word-synced captions with gold active-word highlights.
- Free local Kokoro neural TTS & Whisper word alignment ($0 cost, no API keys).
- Anti-LMK Shield: Flat RAM < 140MB, thermal-safe on mobile and desktop.
